## Formation families approved by the owner

| Family | Geometry and selection intent |
| --- | --- |
| Tectonic | Broad downwarping or elongated rift/fault basins; distinct depth/slope character. |
| Glacial erosion | Cirque bowls or elongated irregular troughs; terrain-compatible cold/high-relief preference when evidence exists. |
| Glacial deposition | Kettle depressions or impounded valleys; varied basin floors and margins. |
| Volcanic | Crater/caldera bowls or lava-impounded valleys; a geometric approximation without geological substrate evidence. |
| River/floodplain | Shallow abandoned channels and crescent oxbow geometry in suitable low-relief drainage settings. |
| Karst/dissolution | Steep sinkhole/collapse bowls; geology is unverified unless supplied by an existing input. |
| Landslide-dammed | Asymmetric elongated upstream valley basins with a natural retaining boundary. |
| Coastal/barrier | Shallow coastal lagoons with appropriate shoreline/sea connectivity constraints. |
| Wind erosion | Broad shallow deflation basins; dry/low-relief preference when accepted climate is available. |
| Meteorite impact | Circular excavation with optional central uplift for larger basin profiles. |
| Biological damming | Small shallow irregular impoundments; no simulated beaver/vegetation authority invented. |

These are controlled terrain-shaping models, not a geological simulation. Procedural placement must still respect the solved containment contour. Existing hand-authored outlines and per-node depths remain authoritative constraints.
