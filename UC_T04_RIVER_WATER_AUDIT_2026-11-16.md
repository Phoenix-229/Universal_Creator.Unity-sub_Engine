## Required experience

A river is a parent with an ordered, editable course of child points. Creating the parent does not create a whole river. The first click establishes its source; the next valid point establishes the first segment. Unset dimensions inherit the preceding authored width/depth, and an override carries forward through subsequent unset points. Existing all-unset courses retain documented hydraulic sizing. Moving points is an author action; fitting must not move them to another valley or another world's terrain.

Manual carve mode builds the owned trench and fitted water after a valid edit. Procedural read mode consumes the accepted trench and fits water without writing heights, holes, details or banks. Disabled digging still permits a validated water surface. Headwaters, lake-fed sources, tributaries and mouths need distinct geometry: a full-depth stamp or a matching height scalar does not establish a natural transition.

Water appearance, local flow, queries, swimming, buoyancy and audio must consume compatible body/position/revision data. These requirements extend to the existing lake, ocean, pond and other water kinds. Realism means coherent, observable behavior with explicit approximations and performance budgets; it is not a claim to simulate full fluid dynamics.

## Sources, assumptions and limits

### Owner refinement — bends, contact, animation and phone cost

The owner further requires the river spline, water mesh and HLSL to behave as one surface through corners and meanders: no crossing ribbons, stacked planes, inverted triangles or apparent climbing over another section. Both manual and procedural modes must keep water in contact with the actual banks and bed; shader motion, normal maps or foam must not conceal unsupported floating geometry. This extends the mandatory UC-T04-06 geometry acceptance and UC-T04-09 displacement/containment acceptance.

Flow cues must travel downstream from the source, including bends and junctions. Rocks and other immersed objects should produce appropriate obstruction, wake, ripple/foam and flow-separation cues. Distinguish static-obstacle cues from moving-object wakes and from a full fluid simulation; inspect the existing ripple/wake owners before extending them. Test surface intersection, obstacle size/depth, duplicate emitters and retirement. Assign geometry/contact to UC-T04-06/09, visual response to UC-T04-08, shared interaction state/events to UC-T04-09A, and their combined acceptance to UC-T04-11.

Wind changes must produce coherent lake/ocean/river surface animation from the existing accepted weather/water state, with different responses for different body types. UC-T04-08/09 must bound displacement at shores and confluences, preserve forward/depth-pass agreement and expose scalable quality through existing owners. UC-T04-11 requires fixed bend/rock/shore scenes under calm and strong wind, CPU/GPU timing, allocations, draw counts, shader variants and sustained thermal/device runs. Use explicit midrange/flagship phone quality budgets and documented degradation; no claim of universally unchanged FPS or freedom from device hangs is supportable without measurement. High detail must come from bounded, scalable work, without another physics/rendering dependency.
